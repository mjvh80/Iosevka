The custom fonts borrow only U+30C4 (KATAKANA LETTER TU), U+30B7 (KATAKANA
LETTER SI), and U+30C3 (KATAKANA LETTER SMALL TU). Their original Iosemka names
are retained. Keep the donor license and this attribution with distributed
fonts.

Configure the donor name, directory, weight files, and accompanying notices in
../extra_glyphs.py. Target weights use the closest available donor weight;
ties select the lighter weight. These glyphs remain upright in italic styles.
